<p style="font-size:30px"> Rocket Engineer - Current Job </p>

I work in the Control Dynamics group which is primarily responsible for development of the rocket's attitude control systems. For each mission, we tune the coast and powered autopilots to ensure adaquate stability margins and good controller performance. We are also resonsible for development of the mission unique flight sequence, collaborating with multiple stakeholder groups to balance competing interests and ensure compliance with all mission requirements. In additon to those tasks, we perform dynamic analysis of several critical flight events (liftoff, stage separation, and spacecraft separation) to demonstrate acceptable clearance margins, attitude pointing errors, and vehicle body rates. For each analysis we perform, we produce a technical memo or presentation to document the results.
